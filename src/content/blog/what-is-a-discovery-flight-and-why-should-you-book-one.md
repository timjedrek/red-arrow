If you are thinking about becoming a pilot, a discovery flight is the best place to start. It is a short, hands-on flight lesson that shows you what it feels like to fly a real airplane. You do not need any experience, and you do not need to commit to flight school yet. A discovery flight helps you decide if flying is right for you.

At **Red Arrow Flight Academy** in **Santa Teresa, New Mexico**, we offer discovery flights for new students from **El Paso**, **Las Cruces**, and the surrounding area.

## What Happens During a Discovery Flight?

A discovery flight is about 30 to 60 minutes long. Before takeoff, you will meet with one of our certified flight instructors. They will explain what to expect and answer any questions you have. Then, you will head out to the airplane, usually a **Cessna 172**, which is a common training aircraft.

Here is what to expect:

- You will sit in the pilot’s seat
- The instructor will go over the controls with you
- You will help taxi the airplane to the runway
- Once in the air, you will get to fly the airplane under supervision
- The instructor will land the airplane for you

This is not a flight where you just sit and watch. You are actually flying with help from an instructor. It is safe, fun, and a great way to try flying before signing up for lessons.

## Who Can Take a Discovery Flight?

Almost anyone can take a discovery flight. You must be at least 14 years old to log flight time toward your pilot license. However, even younger students can still enjoy the experience. Many parents bring their teens to see if aviation could be a good fit.

You do not need a medical certificate. You do not need any books or equipment. You just show up and fly.

## Why Take a Discovery Flight at Red Arrow?

Red Arrow Flight Academy is the only **Part 141 flight school** in the **El Paso** region. We train new pilots from **Santa Teresa**, **Las Cruces**, and even across the border in **Juarez, Mexico**. Our instructors are experienced and friendly. We use well-maintained aircraft and include both simulator and in-air training in our programs.

A discovery flight with Red Arrow is not just a ride. It is a real introduction to flight training. If you decide to keep going, this flight will count toward your private pilot license.

Discovery flights are affordable. Prices vary depending on flight time and aircraft. Contact us directly for current rates. It is a low-cost way to see if you enjoy flying before spending more on full training.
